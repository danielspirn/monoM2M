# Money to Memories — Personas and JTBD Overview

## Purpose

This document defines the core personas and the jobs-to-be-done framing used to guide UX decisions, mocked data, and route validation.

## Core personas

- Teen
- Parent in 2-adult 2-child household
- Single adult male
- Single adult female
- Couple with pet
- Single parent
- Grandparents
- Uncle
- Single elder
- Adult caring for one or two parents
- College student / young adult renter
- Roommates / house-share
- Homeowner / DIY improver
- Gig worker / side-hustle consumer

## JTBD framing

### Core job
Help me understand what I am spending, what I bought, what I own, and what that means in daily life.

### Supporting jobs
- help me find receipts and purchases quickly
- help me see cost drift and habit patterns
- help me manage important Things and docs
- help me connect purchases to people and memories
- help me ask natural-language questions about my data

## Route relevance by persona

### Home
All personas use Home for quick recall and capture.

### Things
Especially important for:
- homeowners
- parents
- single adults managing goods
- elders
- caregivers

### People
Especially important for:
- families
- couples
- single parents
- caregivers
- gift and event-driven users

### Memories
Especially important for:
- families
- couples
- grandparents
- caregivers
- socially active singles

## Validation rule

The mock app must support persona switching in development so route usefulness can be evaluated against different life contexts.
